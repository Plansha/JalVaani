🌊 About the Project

JalVaani is a noise-resilient voice intelligence solution designed to help coastal communities communicate, preserve information, and ensure every story gets heard.

What if AI could understand voices across languages, even in noisy conditions?

That is the vision behind JalVaani.

Coastal communities often communicate in challenging environments where waves, wind, boats, and machinery make speech difficult to understand.

JalVaani explores how AI-powered speech technology can transform spoken audio into meaningful transcripts, evaluate speech recognition performance, identify uncertain phrases, and preserve valuable stories.

Our goal is to make voice communication more reliable, accessible, and meaningful.

---

🚨 Problem Statement

Communication in coastal environments can be affected by:

- 🌊 Background noise from waves and the sea
- 🌬️ Wind and environmental disturbances
- 🚤 Boats, engines, and machinery
- 🗣️ Different languages, accents, and pronunciations
- 🎙️ Unclear speech and incomplete information

These challenges can make speech recognition difficult and increase the risk of misunderstanding.

Traditional speech-to-text systems may produce incorrect or incomplete transcripts when audio quality is poor.

JalVaani aims to address these challenges through speech recognition, transcript evaluation, uncertainty identification, and responsible information preservation.

---

💡 Our Solution

JalVaani combines speech AI, transcript analysis, and user verification into one voice intelligence platform.

The project focuses on four core modules:

🎙️ 1. Speech-to-Text

Converts recorded audio into written transcripts using Gnani AI Speech-to-Text.

Features:

- Audio-to-text conversion
- Multilingual speech processing
- Transcript generation
- Speech interaction support

🔊 2. ShorProof

Evaluates speech recognition performance in quiet and noisy audio environments.

Evaluation metrics:

- WER: Word Error Rate
- CER: Character Error Rate
- Latency: Speech processing time

ShorProof helps evaluate how reliably speech recognition performs under different audio conditions.

🧠 3. ContextEcho

Identifies potentially uncertain transcript phrases for human review.

Instead of blindly trusting every recognized word, ContextEcho helps users identify phrases that may require additional attention.

Its goal is to encourage human verification when speech is unclear.

💾 4. YaadVault

Supports saving and retrieving transcript records to preserve valuable stories and information.

It is designed to help communities preserve:

- Stories
- Experiences
- Community information
- Traditional knowledge
- Valuable spoken information

YaadVault focuses on meaningful information preservation while respecting consent and privacy.

🌍 Our Vision & Impact

JalVaani aims to make voice communication more reliable and inclusive for communities facing challenging communication environments.

Our goals:

- 🎙️ Improve speech accessibility in noisy environments.
- 🌍 Support multilingual voice interactions.
- 🧠 Encourage review of uncertain transcript phrases.
- 💾 Preserve valuable stories and community information.
- 🤝 Promote responsible and consent-based information preservation.

Every voice matters. Every language deserves to be understood. Every story deserves to be preserved.

---

🔐 Responsible AI

JalVaani is designed with responsible AI principles in mind.

- Human Verification: AI-generated transcripts should be reviewed when information is uncertain.
- Privacy: Personal and sensitive information should be handled responsibly.
- Consent: Stories and community information should be preserved with appropriate consent.
- Transparency: Users should understand that speech recognition may produce errors.
- Reliability: Uncertain speech should be highlighted for review whenever possible.
