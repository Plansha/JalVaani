🌊 About the Project

JalVaani is an AI-powered voice intelligence platform designed to help coastal communities understand, verify, and preserve spoken information in noisy environments.

In coastal areas, background noise from waves, wind, boats, and other surroundings can make speech difficult to understand.

Important information such as names, numbers, dates, directions, and community stories may be misunderstood or lost.

JalVaani aims to make voice-based communication more reliable by combining AI-powered speech recognition with transcript verification and consent-based story preservation.

---

💡 Problem Statement

Coastal communities often communicate in challenging environments where background noise can affect speech clarity.

This creates several challenges:

- 🎙️ Speech may be difficult to recognize in noisy environments.
- 📝 Important words and information may be misunderstood.
- 🔍 Incorrect transcripts may go unnoticed.
- 📚 Valuable community stories may be lost.

JalVaani addresses these challenges through AI-assisted speech recognition, transcript review, important-detail verification, and consent-based preservation.

---

✨ Key Features

🎙️ Speech-to-Text

Uses Gnani AI speech technology to convert spoken audio into text.

- Audio transcription
- Language selection
- Transcript display
- Human transcript review

📊 ShorProof

Evaluates transcription quality and processing performance.

- Word Error Rate (WER)
- Character Error Rate (CER)
- Processing latency
- Clean and noisy audio comparison

🔍 ContextEcho

Highlights potentially uncertain transcript phrases so users can review and correct them.

- Identifies phrases for review
- Supports manual transcript correction
- Encourages human verification
- Helps users avoid blindly trusting AI-generated text

✅ CriticalDetails

Helps verify important information against reference information.

- Names
- Numbers
- Dates
- Money
- Directions

📚 YaadVault

Allows users to save and retrieve transcripts and stories with consent.

- Transcript preservation
- Story retrieval
- Community knowledge preservation
- Consent-based storage

🌍 Impact

JalVaani aims to:

- Improve access to voice-based information in noisy environments.
- Help users identify and correct potentially misunderstood speech.
- Make important spoken details easier to verify.
- Preserve community stories through consent-based transcript storage.

---

🔐 Responsible AI

JalVaani encourages human review of uncertain transcripts and consent-based preservation of stories.

AI-generated transcripts should be reviewed before being treated as verified information.

Important information should be checked against reliable reference details whenever possible.
